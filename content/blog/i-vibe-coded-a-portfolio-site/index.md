This past weekend I had a random idea to let claude build me a personal, technical portfolio website. I'd
recently picked up the domain [bkmontgomery.dev](https://bkmontgomery.dev), and this seemed like a good use-case.

Yes, there's [/projects/](https://bradmontgomery.net/projects/), and I have six of these pinned to my
[github profile](https://github.com/bradmontgomery), but that's not everything I want to highlight.

So yeah... **I one-shotted a personal portfolio site**.

I used Claude Code CLI with Fable 5, and I _really_ wish I'd kept the prompt (maybe it's in my claude history
but I don't have access to that right now), but it included the phrases neon techno and steampunk, and I'm actually
super happy about the results.

Check it out at [bkmontgomery.dev](https://bkmontgomery.dev).
